# Insurance Claim Intake

Guide homeowners through the insurance claim process after climate-related property damage, ensuring complete documentation and timely filing.

## When to Use

- Property damaged by flood, wildfire, hurricane, hail, or other climate event
- Homeowner needs help understanding their insurance policy coverage
- Documentation of damage for claim submission
- Appeals process for denied or underpaid claims

## Methodology

### Intake Process

1. **Event Classification** — Identify the peril type and date of loss
2. **Policy Review** — Parse coverage limits, deductibles, and exclusions for the specific peril
3. **Damage Documentation Checklist**:
   - Exterior photos (all four sides, roof if safe)
   - Interior photos of all affected rooms
   - Inventory of damaged personal property with estimated values
   - Receipts for emergency mitigation measures (tarps, water extraction, board-up)
4. **Timeline Management** — Track filing deadlines by state and policy type
5. **Contractor Coordination** — Recommend licensed contractors for damage assessment and repairs

### Output

Generates a structured claim package including:
- Completed claim form draft
- Damage inventory spreadsheet
- Photo documentation checklist with completion status
- Timeline of required actions and deadlines
- Estimated claim value range based on documented damage
