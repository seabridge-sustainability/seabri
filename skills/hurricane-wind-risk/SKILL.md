## When to Use

Activate when a user asks about hurricane risk, tropical cyclone exposure, typhoon impact, storm surge, wind damage to property, or climate-driven changes to tropical storm frequency and intensity.

## Methodology

1. Determine the location's historical hurricane return periods and landfall probability using HURDAT2 or IBTrACS track data.
2. Classify current and projected risk under Saffir-Simpson categories (1–5) for the relevant basin.
3. Apply TCFD Physical Risk taxonomy: acute (individual events) and chronic (trend toward more intense storms under warming).
4. Assess ISSB IFRS S2 exposure: identify which assets and revenues are in high-wind or storm-surge zones.
5. Evaluate compounding risks: sea-level rise increasing storm surge reach, warming SSTs increasing rapid intensification probability.
6. Provide risk rating: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL with confidence and time horizon.

## Output

- Risk narrative (2–4 sentences) covering basin-specific historical frequency, projected intensity change, and key vulnerabilities
- Key metrics: 100-year return period wind speed, storm surge height, projected Category 4–5 frequency change by 2050
- TCFD and ISSB S2 disclosure bullet points
- Recommended resilience measures (structural hardening, elevation, evacuation planning, parametric insurance)

## Active Emergency Escalation Protocol

If the user reports an active or imminent hurricane emergency (e.g. storm making landfall, evacuation underway, structural damage occurring):

1. Immediately provide evacuation guidance, shelter-in-place protocols, and emergency contact numbers.
2. Escalate to notify_emergency if life safety is at risk.
3. Do not defer to scenario analysis — switch to real-time safety guidance.
4. Ask: "Is there an active storm threat right now? If yes, reply YES EMERGENCY."
