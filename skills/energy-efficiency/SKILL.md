# Energy Efficiency Advisor

Analyse energy consumption patterns and recommend efficiency improvements with estimated savings and available incentives.

## When to Use

- Homeowner or business wants to reduce energy costs and emissions
- ESRS E1 Climate Change disclosure requiring energy efficiency metrics
- CDP Climate questionnaire sections on energy consumption
- GRI 302 Energy standard reporting
- IRA tax credit and rebate eligibility assessment

## Methodology

### Energy Audit Process

1. **Baseline Assessment** — Collect utility bills (12 months minimum), building characteristics, occupancy patterns
2. **Benchmarking** — Compare against ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager benchmarks for building type and climate zone
3. **Opportunity Identification** — Rank improvements by payback period:
   - Envelope (insulation, air sealing, windows)
   - HVAC (heat pumps, smart thermostats)
   - Lighting (LED conversion)
   - Appliances (ENERGY STAR rated replacements)
   - Renewables (solar PV, battery storage)

### Incentive Matching

Cross-references available incentives:
- Federal IRA tax credits (25C, 25D, 45L)
- State utility rebate programs
- Local weatherisation assistance (WAP)
- PACE financing availability

### Output Format

Returns structured recommendations with:
- Estimated annual energy savings (kWh, therms)
- Estimated cost savings (USD/year)
- Available incentives and net cost after rebates
- GHG reduction potential (tCO2e/year)
- Simple payback period for each measure
