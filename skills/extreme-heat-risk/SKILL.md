## When to Use

Activate when a user asks about heat stress, high-temperature events, heat waves, wet-bulb temperature thresholds, or the physical risk of extreme heat to assets, workers, agriculture, or infrastructure.

## Methodology

1. Assess the location's historical temperature trends and projected warming trajectories under IPCC scenarios (SSP2-4.5, SSP5-8.5).
2. Evaluate wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT) and heat index thresholds relevant to the asset or population.
3. Apply TCFD Physical Risk taxonomy: classify as chronic (rising mean temperatures) or acute (extreme heat events).
4. Cross-reference with ISSB IFRS S2 climate-related physical risk disclosure requirements.
5. Identify exposed assets: outdoor workers, data centers, livestock, crops, and building cooling loads.
6. Provide risk rating: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL with confidence and time horizon.

## Output

- Risk narrative (2–4 sentences) covering likelihood, severity, and time horizon
- Quantitative indicators where available (e.g. number of days >35°C per year by 2050)
- TCFD-aligned disclosure bullet points
- Recommended adaptation measures (cooling infrastructure, operational scheduling, insurance)

## Active Emergency Escalation Protocol

If the user indicates a current heat emergency (e.g. workers collapsing, livestock dying, cooling system failure during extreme heat):

1. Immediately surface emergency cooling resources and emergency contact numbers.
2. Escalate to notify_emergency protocol if life safety is at risk.
3. Do not defer to scheduled risk reporting — switch to real-time guidance mode.
4. Ask: "Is anyone experiencing heat illness symptoms right now? If yes, reply YES EMERGENCY."
