## When to Use

Activate when a user asks about water scarcity, drought conditions, water stress, low river flows, groundwater depletion, or the impact of drought on agriculture, supply chains, or asset operations.

## Methodology

1. Identify the location's current drought status using standardised indices (SPI-12, SPEI-12, PDSI).
2. Map the asset or operation against watershed boundaries and aquifer recharge zones.
3. Assess physical water risk using TCFD chronic (multi-year drying trends) and acute (flash drought) categories.
4. Apply TNFD LEAP approach: Locate assets relative to freshwater-dependent ecosystems; Evaluate dependencies and impacts on freshwater ecosystems; Assess material risks and opportunities; Prepare TNFD-aligned disclosures.
5. Quantify exposure: water withdrawal volumes vs. basin availability under baseline and stress scenarios.
6. Provide risk rating: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL with confidence and time horizon.

## Output

- Risk narrative (2–4 sentences) covering water stress trajectory, sector-specific exposure, and time horizon
- Key indicators: basin-level water depletion ratio, annual variability, projected change by 2030/2050
- TNFD LEAP step summary for disclosure
- Recommended adaptation measures (water recycling, drought-resistant crops, supplier diversification)

## Active Emergency Escalation Protocol

If the user reports an active drought emergency (e.g. municipal water cut-off, crop failure in progress, operational shutdown due to water shortage):

1. Surface nearest emergency water supply contacts and government drought assistance programmes.
2. Escalate to notify_emergency if human health or critical infrastructure is at risk.
3. Do not defer to long-term scenario planning — switch to immediate operational guidance.
4. Ask: "Is water access cut off right now? If yes, reply YES EMERGENCY."
