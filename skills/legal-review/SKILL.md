# Sustainability Legal Review

Review sustainability disclosures and corporate reports for legal compliance with major ESG regulatory frameworks.

## When to Use

- Pre-publication review of sustainability or annual reports
- Due diligence on ESG claims for investment decisions
- Compliance gap analysis against new or upcoming regulations
- Greenwashing risk assessment for marketing materials

## Frameworks Covered

### Climate Disclosure
- **TCFD** — Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures: governance, strategy, risk management, metrics & targets
- **ISSB S2** — Climate-related disclosures under IFRS Sustainability Standards

### Real Estate / Infrastructure
- **GRESB** — Global Real Estate Sustainability Benchmark alignment for property portfolios

### Nature & Biodiversity
- **TNFD** — Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures: LEAP approach (Locate, Evaluate, Assess, Prepare)

### EU Regulation
- **CSDDD** — Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive: value chain due diligence obligations, adverse impact identification, remediation

## Methodology

1. **Document Parsing** — Extract claims, metrics, targets, and forward-looking statements
2. **Framework Mapping** — Map each disclosure element to applicable framework requirements
3. **Gap Analysis** — Identify missing required disclosures per framework
4. **Risk Flagging** — Highlight statements that may constitute greenwashing or lack substantiation
5. **Recommendation** — Prioritised list of disclosure improvements

## Limitations

- Not a substitute for qualified legal advice
- Regulatory interpretation may vary by jurisdiction
- Does not cover sector-specific regulations (e.g., EU Taxonomy technical screening criteria)
