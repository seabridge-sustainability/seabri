# OpenSeaBri Research Program

Edit this file to direct what OpenSeaBri researches autonomously.
The agent runs research cycles against this agenda, stores findings
in research/findings/, and updates its skills based on what it learns.
Each cycle runs for approximately 30 minutes.

## Current Research Agenda

### Physical Risk: What Is Actually Changing and Where

- Flood risk trends in US metros: which cities face the fastest increase in frequency and severity
- Wildfire perimeter expansion: how far fire risk geography has moved in the last decade and projections for 2035–2050
- Heat stress thresholds: where outdoor work and daily life are becoming dangerous and when
- Coastal communities: the gap between official flood maps and actual current risk

### The Reporting Landscape in Plain Language

- What small and medium businesses actually need to do right now vs what is coming in 3–5 years
- What lenders are now asking borrowers about climate
- What institutional investors expect from asset managers today
- How requirements differ by country and jurisdiction

### Nature and Food Systems

- How water scarcity is affecting food production regions
- Which agricultural areas face the highest climate and nature risk
- What nature-based income programs are available to landowners
- How biodiversity loss is creating supply chain risks in key sectors

### The Economics of Sustainability Action

- Home resilience upgrades: which have the best payback
- Commercial building energy efficiency: what the data shows
- Solar and storage at different scales: when it makes financial sense
- Nature-based solutions: what generates real income vs theoretical

## Research Protocol

Each cycle:
1. Search for recent sources (< 6 months old where possible)
2. Synthesize findings into 3–5 plain-language takeaways
3. Identify what this means for different types of users
4. Store in research/findings/YYYY-MM-DD.md
5. Update relevant skill files if new methodology is encountered
6. Flag outdated information in existing skills for revision

Quality metric: are the findings actionable for a real person making a real decision? If not, dig deeper.
