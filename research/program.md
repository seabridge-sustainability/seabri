## Research Agenda

### Physical Climate Risk
- Compound flooding events: when storm surge and rainfall combine — risk for coastal cities
- Wildfire smoke health impacts and economic costs beyond property damage
- Heat stress thresholds for outdoor agricultural labor — crops and workers
- Permafrost thaw acceleration and its downstream effects on Arctic infrastructure
- Drought stress and groundwater depletion rates in the US Southwest and Central Valley
- Hurricane intensification: rapid intensification events and forecast skill gaps

### Transition Risk and Policy
- Current status of US state-level carbon pricing and emissions trading programs
- EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) — coverage, phase-in, and cost implications
- IRA clean energy credit flow: which technologies are seeing largest uptake
- Scope 3 disclosure rules: SEC climate rule status and alternative state-level rules
- Building decarbonization codes: which states have adopted stretch codes
- Natural gas infrastructure stranding risk — timeline and asset value implications

### Nature and Biodiversity
- TNFD final framework: what physical and transition risks it covers that TCFD does not
- High biodiversity value areas: deforestation-free supply chain mapping tools
- Freshwater biodiversity loss rates and implications for water-dependent industries
- Ocean acidification and coral bleaching: updated projections for 2025-2030
- Soil carbon sequestration: current scientific consensus on measurement and permanence
- Pollinator collapse risk: geographic concentration and agricultural dependency

### Insurance and Financial Risk
- Property insurance market withdrawal from wildfire and flood zones: state by state
- FEMA National Flood Insurance Program: solvency, reform status, and rate trajectory
- Climate stress testing: central bank requirements across major economies
- Mortgage market exposure to physical climate risk — current bank disclosures
- Carbon credit quality crisis: key integrity failures and what they mean for buyers
- Climate litigation risk: recent cases and implications for corporate disclosure

### Solutions and Adaptation
- Community solar programs: access, pricing, and grid interconnection bottlenecks
- Green building retrofits: payback periods at current energy prices
- Managed retreat programs: where they exist, how they are funded, what actually works
- Regenerative agriculture adoption rates and barriers — what is stopping the transition
- Industrial heat decarbonization: hydrogen vs. electrification vs. heat pumps by sector
- Water efficiency technology for agriculture: precision irrigation and soil sensors

### Effective Query Patterns
- For regulatory topics: include "2024 2025" to get recent developments — Tavily returns better results
- For scientific topics: include the source organization (NOAA, IPCC, First Street Foundation) to get cited data
- For financial topics: ask about specific sectors (real estate, agriculture, infrastructure) rather than broad economy

### Quality Observations
- Tavily search performs better than Anthropic-only for topics with recent policy developments
- For scientific topics without recent news, Anthropic knowledge base often has better depth than web snippets
- Compound questions ("X and Y implications") tend to produce lower-quality synthesis — split them
